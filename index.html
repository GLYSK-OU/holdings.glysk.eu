GLYSK Holdings is a venture builder and early-stage investor. We work with founders at the moment of maximum potential. Before the model is fully proven. Before the market catches on.

We don't just provide capital. We work inside the company. Validating assumptions, refining go-to-market strategy, and building the foundations for sustainable scaling.

Beyond funding, we bring hands-on mentorship, operational depth, and a network built across fintech, B2B SaaS, and AI-enabled industries. We stay close through every stage of growth.

Early-stage is the hardest stage. We're designed for it.

Investment Thesis

Where we look.
How we work.

We focus where advisory depth and capital can move together. Our thesis is tight by design. We'd rather be the right partner than the most available one.

Stage

Pre-seed and seed. We enter when the risk is highest and the potential is greatest. We don't wait for traction. We help create it.

Sectors

Fintech, B2B SaaS, and AI-enabled technology. Businesses with structural leverage, recurring economics, and a clear path to European and global scale.

Geography

EU-based founders, primarily. We know the regulatory landscape, the talent markets, and the investor ecosystem across the continent.

Model

Active involvement, not passive capital. We embed in the team, contribute to strategy, and bring the GLYSK Advisory network to every portfolio company.
